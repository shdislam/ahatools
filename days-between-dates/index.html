📅 Days Between Dates

Countdown to an exam, wedding, deadline — anything.

About this tool

A precise days-between-dates counter for exam countdowns, project deadlines, visa stays, pregnancy weeks or "how long have we been together" — handling leap years and different month lengths correctly, which mental math always gets wrong.

How to use it

  1. Pick the two dates — order doesn't matter, negatives are impossible.
  2. You get total days plus a years/months/days breakdown and week count.
  3. Counting age in total days? Use your birth date as the start.

FAQ

Are leap years counted?

Yes — real calendar arithmetic, so Feb 29, century rules and 31-day months are all handled exactly. Example: Feb 28 → Mar 1, 2024 correctly equals 2 days.

Does it count business days?

Total days only for now. Quick manual rule: divide by 7 and subtract ~4.3 weekends per month for rough working-day estimates.
