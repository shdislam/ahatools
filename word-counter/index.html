About this tool

Students checking essay limits, writers meeting deadlines, and anyone posting where characters are capped — this counter shows words, characters, sentences and estimated reading time live as you type or paste. Your text stays on your device; nothing is uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type into the box — stats update instantly.
  2. Characters count includes spaces (what most forms and social platforms measure).
  3. Reading time assumes an average adult pace of ~200 words per minute.

FAQ

Do characters include spaces?

Yes — the character figure counts every letter, space and punctuation mark, which is what Twitter/X, SMS gates and meta-description limits actually enforce.

How accurate is reading time?

It's a solid estimate for general audiences at 200 wpm. Technical content runs slower (~150 wpm), casual posts faster. Writing something long? Track sentence variety too — short sentences read faster than the math suggests.
