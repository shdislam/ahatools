🏦 Loan EMI Calculator

See your exact monthly payment before you borrow.

About this tool

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount you pay a bank every month on a loan. This calculator shows that payment before you sign anything — plus the total interest you'll pay over the full duration, which banks don't highlight upfront.

How to use it

  1. Enter loan amount, annual interest rate and duration in years.
  2. Check the monthly EMI — compare it against your monthly income (most advisors say keep it under 40%).
  3. Look at Total interest: try shortening the duration and watch how much money it saves.

FAQ

What is the EMI formula?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P = principal, r = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = months. It's the standard reducing-balance formula used by banks worldwide.

How can I pay less interest overall?

Two proven levers: shorter tenure (biggest saving) and prepayments early in the loan, when most of each EMI is still interest. Compare scenarios above before committing.
