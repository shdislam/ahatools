⚖️ BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index using centimeters and kilograms.

About this tool

BMI (Body Mass Index) is the screening measure recommended by the World Health Organization to check whether your weight is in a healthy range for your height. It takes seconds, needs only a tape-measure-free height value in centimetres and weight in kilograms.

How to use it

  1. Enter height in cm (e.g. 170) and weight in kg (e.g. 65).
  2. Your BMI shows instantly with its WHO category: under 18.5 underweight · 18.5–24.9 normal · 25–29.9 overweight · 30+ obese.
  3. The result also shows the healthy weight range for your exact height.

FAQ

What is the BMI formula?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²). For example 65 kg at 1.70 m → 65 ÷ 2.89 = 22.5.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not perfectly — muscle weighs more than fat, so very muscular people can score "overweight" while being healthy. Treat BMI as a quick screening number, not a medical diagnosis; for body composition ask a professional.
