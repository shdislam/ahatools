🎂 Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to get your exact age.

About this tool

This age calculator works the same way official offices do — full calendar years first, then leftover months, then days. People use it for birthday planning, school/admission forms, visa and job applications where you must state age in exact years, months and days.

How to use it

  1. Pick your date of birth in the box above.
  2. Press Calculate Age — no clicking through dropdowns needed.
  3. Want total days lived instead? Use our Days Between Dates tool with your birth date as the start.

FAQ

How is age calculated exactly?

It counts complete calendar years between your birth date and today, then remaining whole months, then remaining days — borrowing days from the previous month when needed (e.g. from Aug 30 to Aug 24 next year gives 11 months and 25 days).

Why do some sites show a different age?

Sites that divide total days by 365.25 get slightly wrong month/day values. Calendar-accurate counting like this one matches what appears on official documents.
