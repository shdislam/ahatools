🖼️ Image Compressor

Your photo never leaves your device — everything happens in your browser.

About this tool

Photos from phones are often 3–8 MB — too big for forms, email limits and slow websites. This compressor shrinks them to a fraction of the size, and because everything happens on your device via HTML Canvas, your pictures are never uploaded anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose an image (JPG or PNG).
  2. Pick quality: 60–75% is the sweet spot — visually identical for photos at half the size.
  3. Compress & Download — very large images are also resized down to max 1600px, perfect for web use.

FAQ

Which quality should I choose?

70% suits most photos. Go 85–95% for prints or text-heavy images; below 50% you'll start seeing blur around sharp edges.

Why did my PNG get bigger/smaller than expected?

Output is JPEG, which compresses photographs extremely well but flattens transparency. For logos with transparent backgrounds, keep the original PNG instead.
