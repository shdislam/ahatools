Three most-used percentage calculations.

About this tool

Percentages trip everyone up because there are three different everyday questions — "what's X% of Y", "X is what percent of Y" and "how much did it change". This page answers all three live as you type, no button needed.

The three calculations

  1. X% of Y — tips, taxes, commissions. Example: 10% of 200 = 20.
  2. X is what % of Y — test scores, market share. Example: 45 of 200 = 22.5%.
  3. % change — price rises, growth reports. Example: 80 → 100 = +25% increase.

FAQ

What is the percentage change formula?

((new − old) ÷ |old|) × 100. Going from 80 to 100 is (20 ÷ 80) × 100 = 25% up; the reverse is −20%, because percentages aren't symmetric.

Where does this help daily?

Restaurant bills, exam marks, salary hike offers, discount stacking (see our Discount Calculator) and loan interest comparisons.
