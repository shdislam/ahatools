Pick a grade and credits for each course.

About this tool

This GPA calculator computes your credit-weighted grade point average on the standard 4.00 scale used by most universities in the US, Bangladesh, Pakistan and beyond. Credits matter: an A in a 4-credit course moves your GPA more than an A in a 1-credit lab.

How to use it

  1. Pick a letter grade and credit hours for each course.
  2. Add more rows for extra courses with one click.
  3. Your GPA appears instantly weighted by credits.

FAQ

What's the formula?

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) ÷ Σ(credits). Grade points: A=4.00, A−=3.70, B+=3.30, B=3.00, C+=2.30, C=2.00, D=1.00, F=0.

Is GPA the same as CGPA?

GPA covers one semester; CGPA averages all semesters so far. Calculate each semester here, then average them credit-weighted for CGPA. Some universities use different scales — check yours before applying scholarships.
