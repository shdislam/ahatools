🍽️ Tip Calculator

Bill splitting with tip, done in seconds.

About this tool

Dinner with friends shouldn't end with five people doing mental math over one receipt. Enter the bill, choose tip %, set how many are splitting — everyone sees their exact share including tip in one number.

How to use it

  1. Enter total bill amount and number of people.
  2. Slide the tip (or tap 5/10/15/20% quick chips).
  3. Per-person amount shows instantly — no rounding arguments left.

FAQ

How much should I tip?

It's cultural: 15–20% is standard at US restaurants; in many countries including Bangladesh service is included and tipping is optional appreciation for good service. The slider covers whatever your situation calls for.

Should the tip be split equally?

Equal splitting per person is the default here because it's how groups usually settle. If one person ordered significantly more, calculate their share separately with our Percentage Calculator.
